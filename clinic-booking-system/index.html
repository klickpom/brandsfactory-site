Questions clinics ask before they buy

What is a clinic booking system?

A clinic booking system is software plus a clinic website that lets patients pick a service, day, and time, locks that slot, and notifies the practice. Brands Factory’s version also sends WhatsApp confirmation and reminder messages and includes a staff panel.

Is this a monthly subscription like Cliniko or Calendly?

No. It is a one-time project. You are not locked into a per-seat monthly booking SaaS. Hosting for the first year is included on the Egypt packages. After that, hosting is a normal site cost, not a booking-license ransom.

Can clinics outside Egypt use it?

Yes. The product is a website. WhatsApp is used by clinics in Africa, the Middle East, Latin America, South Asia, and parts of Europe. We quote international work as a one-time project. Language, currency labels, and map location follow the clinic, not Egypt by default.

How fast can we go live?

The first launch is delivered within 24 hours after we have complete clinic details: name, hours, services, WhatsApp, and address.

Is the demo fake?

No. brandsfactory.site/demo/clinic is a working booking flow. Bookings are stored in the browser so you can see the panel fill up. It is a demonstration of the product, not a multi-clinic cloud account.

Will this make us rank on Google and in ChatGPT answers?

The clinic site is built so search engines and answer engines can read specialty, location, hours, and booking steps. Ranking still depends on the clinic’s name, area, reviews, and whether anyone else already owns those queries. We set the technical floor. We do not sell fake #1 guarantees.
